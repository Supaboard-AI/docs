## New

- **Group related columns under one heading.** Column groups and an aggregate footer help readers understand a wide table and its totals. [Table settings](/dashboards/widgets/table-widget).

## Improved

- **Set up the view your readers need.** Open row groups by default, choose group-header colors, and use conditional text colors to highlight values.

## Fixed

- Accepting an SQL autocomplete suggestion no longer duplicates or joins characters incorrectly in the affected editor flow. [Query Bench](/query-bench/overview).

- Building and testing automation branches handles branch state more reliably.
