## New

- **Build a dashboard from a request.** Describe what you want to see and review the proposed plan before the dashboard is generated. The plan carries its title and summary into the dashboard. [Create a dashboard](/dashboards/how-to/create-a-dashboard).

- **Find actions with a keyboard shortcut.** Open the command palette with **⌘K** on Mac or **Ctrl+K** on Windows to find pages and common actions.

- **Share a conversation or a specific answer.** Sharing controls give teammates a link to the chat, with message links opening at the relevant answer.

## Improved

- **Work with PDFs and presentations in context.** Preview these files inside Supaboard. AI chat can read their text and retain previously attached files for follow-up questions.

- **Keep large pivot tables readable.** Group by a second field and show the top results with the remainder collected into “Other.” [Table settings](/dashboards/widgets/table-widget).

- **Ask how to use Supaboard.** AI chat can consult the documentation to help with product questions.

## Fixed

- Inviting someone who already has a Supaboard account no longer fails in the existing-user invitation flow.
