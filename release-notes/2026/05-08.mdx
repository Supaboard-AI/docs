## Improved

- **Review a seat charge before changing access.** A charge preview helps you understand the cost before promoting a workspace member. Invoices appear newest first. [Manage workspace access](/workspace/invite-and-manage-access).

- **Paste an attachment into a question.** Add supporting material directly to your message without starting from the upload picker.

## Fixed

- Database connection forms handle passwords containing special characters more reliably, including PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Snowflake connections. [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect-a-data-source).

- Filters carry through more reliably when opening underlying records from tables and KPI widgets.

- Invoices that previously failed to open can now be viewed.
