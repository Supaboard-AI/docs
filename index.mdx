<span id="what-is-supaboard" />

Supaboard is an AI analyst for your business. Connect your data, ask a question in plain English, and work with the answer as a chart, dashboard, report, or interactive site. You don't need to write SQL to get started.

<Card title="New to Supaboard?" icon="rocket" href="/getting-started/quickstart" horizontal>
  Follow the quickstart to ask your first question and turn the answer into something you can use.
</Card>

  <Card title="Create and share" icon="chart-simple" href="/artifacts/overview">
