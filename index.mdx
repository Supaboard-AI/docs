# What is Supaboard?

Supaboard is an AI data analyst for your business. Connect your databases and apps, and ask questions in plain English — *"why did revenue drop?"*, *"what's driving churn?"* — and get answers with the actual numbers behind them, not just a chart to interpret yourself.

What that looks like in practice:

- **Agents trained on your business.** You build [agents](/agents) that carry your own definitions, rules, and knowledge — so "active customer" means what it means at your company, not what a generic model guesses.
- **Answers that explain themselves.** When a metric moves, an agent doesn't stop at showing you. It investigates why it changed and suggests what to do next.
- **No SQL needed.** Plain English is the primary interface. Agents find trends and anomalies across your connected sources on their own — though when you *want* to write SQL, the [Query Builder](/query-builder) is a full editor.
