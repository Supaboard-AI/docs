# Ask your first question

After setting up your workspace and adding a data source, you land in Ask Agent with a default agent named **Stella**. Stella answers questions about your database — metrics, trends, or simply what's in there. A good first message:

<Prompt description={`Tell me about this data`} icon="user-robot-xmarks" actions={["copy"]}>
  Tell me about this data
</Prompt>

Stella queries your database, reads the relevant tables, flags anything that looks unusual, and gives you an overview of what your data contains. It's the quickest way to see what the agents can do before you start shaping one of your own.

Once you've created your own agents, switch between them from the selector in the chat interface.

# Create your first agent

Click **Agents** in the sidebar to open the agent explorer. From here you can create and customise your own agents:

- Set the basics: a name, a description of what the agent does, and whether it should improve itself as you ask it questions
- Scope its data — which data sources and tables it can see
- Give it a master ruleset: the conventions your business follows
- Add knowledge as CSV, PDF, or doc files that explain your business
- Control what each table contains, column by column

<Note>
  The "Table Configuration" setting is designed for data analysts to further finetune the agent. If you are unsure, leave them untouched.
</Note>
