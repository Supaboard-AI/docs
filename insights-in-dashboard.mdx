---
title: "Insights in Dashboard"
mode: "wide"
---

When Supaboard generates insights for a dashboard widget, the result is a short read built from that widget's numbers. It has three parts.

**Overview.** A brief summary of the data in the widget: the main patterns and trends, and what they mean for your business, in plain language.

**Insights.** Two or three standout points. Each one has a short title you can scan, the point itself — what's important, unusual, or worth acting on — and a tone marker (positive, negative, or neutral) so you know at a glance how to read it.

**Scope.** A line or two stating which period the numbers cover and which filters or conditions apply, so you know exactly what the overview and insights are based on.

Insights stay tied to the numbers in that one widget — and, when you've asked a question, to your question as well.
