## Connect Supaboard to Claude

Supaboard is now available as a **connector for Claude**. Add it once and ask questions about your dashboards and data straight from a Claude conversation.

- Secure sign-in with OAuth — you pick the workspace and agent to connect, and access is strictly read-only
- Answers come back with fully rendered charts that match your dashboard's theme and accent color, not just text
- Built on MCP, with more chat surfaces (ChatGPT, Slack) planned on the same foundation
