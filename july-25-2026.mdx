## Introducing Databook

Databook is a new way to turn your data into a fully hosted, shareable mini-site — a **datasite**. Describe what you want, and Supaboard's AI analyst plans, builds, and hosts it for you.

- **Chat with the analyst**: Build, plan, or just ask questions about your data from a chat panel with live thinking steps, so you can follow what the AI is doing while it works
- **Live preview**: Watch your datasite take shape in a side-by-side preview as it's built
- **Code view**: A full file tree with syntax highlighting lets you inspect and edit the generated files directly, including the site's plan and data glossary
- **Select & edit**: Click any element in the preview and ask the AI to change just that part
- **Publish & embed**: Publish your datasite when it's ready and embed it anywhere with a generated embed snippet

Databook is available from the sidebar. This is just the start — expect rapid improvements over the coming weeks.
