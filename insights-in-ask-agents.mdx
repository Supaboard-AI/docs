When you ask a question in [Ask Agents](/ask-agents), the answer comes in one of two styles, matching the mode selector in the composer: **Concise** and **Deep Dive**.

## Concise answers

A short block of text that answers the question directly — the main message in a few lines, with key figures mentioned where they help, but no full tables or long lists. Written in plain business language. This is the style for quick follow-ups and simple lookups, where speed matters more than depth.

## Deep Dive answers

A structured mini-report:

1. The direct answer to your question, first
2. Sections that each take one idea — the overall trend, the key drivers, an important segment
3. A short summary at the end with 3–5 takeaways

Each section is linked to the widget whose numbers it's based on, so you can check any claim against the data behind it. Two extra pieces ride along:

- a small **definitions** widget that explains specialist terms in plain words
- a **follow-up questions** section with three next questions based on what was just explained — a time period to drill into, a channel, a group of customers

Both styles stay close to the question you asked and explain the why and how behind the numbers, not just the values. Switching between them is covered in [Ask Agents](/ask-agents) — the mode selector, and the "Go Deeper" shortcut on a Concise answer.
